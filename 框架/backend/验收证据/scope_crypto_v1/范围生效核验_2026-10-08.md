```json

  "coins": 526,
  "crypto_index": ["BTCDOMUSDT", "ALLUSDT"],
  "old_minus_coins": ["ALLUSDT", "BTCDOMUSDT"],
  "coins_minus_old": [],
  "coins_intersect_index": [],
  "okx_in_binance": [],
```

- `coins − old_items = ∅`（新范围没有引入旧清单之外的新标的，只是**去掉** 2 个指数）；
